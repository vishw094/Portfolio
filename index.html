05 — APPROACHHOW I WORK

        
Five rules.

        
    
          
  1. 01
    Start from the decision
    Before I touch the data I ask what someone will do differently with the answer. If nothing changes, it's not the right question yet.
    2. 
          
  2. 02
    Pipelines before models
    A model is only as good as the data that reaches it, on time, every day. I make the boring part reliable first.
    3. 
          
  3. 03
    Beat the baseline first
    The simple model sets the bar. Fancy architecture has to earn its place against it.
    4. 
          
  4. 04
    Production is the test set
    Holdout accuracy is a start. Success rate in production, and whether people come back, is the proof.
    5. 
          
  5. 05
    Explain the why
    A number isn't an answer until it says why it moved and what to do about it.
